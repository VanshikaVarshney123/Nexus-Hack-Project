ABOUT THE HACKATHON

Built by Developers, for Future Innovators

Organized under the spirit of the GDG Student Developer Community, NEXUS HACK 2026 bridges the gap between abstract generative AI prompts and robust, real-world pixel deployments.

💡

Our Mission

We believe prompting is the new programming language, but true engineering lies in transforming raw intelligence into accessible, intuitive, and high-impact digital experiences. Our goal is to empower student builders to ship functional solutions within 36 hours.

🚀

Who Can Participate?

Whether you are a first-year student writing your first HTML tag, an experienced AI researcher, a UI/UX designer, or an aspiring founder—NEXUS HACK offers equal ground, mentorship, and resources for all skill tiers.

👥
